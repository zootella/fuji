# Three video window spikes

## What the research found about the buttons and the corners

- **The Mac's geometry, measured on the Mac mini with Sequoia 15.7.4.** AppKit makes each button a frame 14 by 16 points. In a standard 28-point title bar, and in a window whose title bar is hidden over full-size content, the circles are 12 points across, centered 14 points down and 14, 34 and 54 points in from the left. A toolbar moves them: centered 19 points in and down in a 38-point compact bar, and 26 in a 52-point unified one. A research session measured this on 2026-10-21 by having AppKit draw its real buttons into memory, with no window shown.
- **The Mac's colors on Sequoia**, from the same measurement, in sRGB. Idle in light: close #FF5F57 with a ring of #E44139, minimize #FEBC2E with #E3A00F, zoom #28C840 with #0AAC22; in dark the same fills with no ring. The hover glyphs in light: × #7E0707, − #B3710C, and the zoom glyph #036804; in dark the × is #A10707. Pressed in light, about three quarters of each fill: #BF4741, #BE8D23, #1E9630. Pressed in dark, lighter instead: #FF8D85, #FFEA5C, #56F66E. Inactive in light: #E5E5E5 with a ring near #C3C3C3. The pointer over any of the three shows the glyphs on all three, which AppKit decides through `_mouseInGroup:`, a private method on the window's frame view.
- **Tahoe and Golden Gate moved them.** Tahoe's are 14-point lights 23 points apart, the first centered 16 points in and down in a 32-point bar, by developers' reports ([bb #5459](https://github.com/get-bb/bb/pull/5459), [IINA's Titlebar.swift](https://github.com/iina/iina/blob/HEAD/iina/Titlebar.swift)), with the layout following the SDK an application is built with, per [Zed #45351](https://github.com/zed-industries/zed/pull/45351). macOS 27, Golden Gate, the current release since mid-September 2026 ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/28/apple-releases-macos-27-0-1/)), redesigned them again ([Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/08/18/golden-gate-27-window-controls)), and nobody has published their geometry.
- **No ready-made drawing matches the Mac,** and Apple's Human Interface Guidelines say "don't try to replicate the system-provided appearance. Doing so without perfectly matching the system's look and behavior can make your app feel broken." The closest is tauri-controls' SVGs ([macos.tsx](https://github.com/agmmnn/tauri-controls/blob/master/apps/tauri-controls/src/tauri-controls/controls/macos.tsx)). AppKit's `standardWindowButton(_:for:)` makes the running macOS's own buttons for any view, and on a window with no title bar their group hover takes the private `_mouseInGroup:`.
- **Windows draws its buttons from bitmaps, not a font**, a glyph bitmap per DPI in the theme's image atlas ([msstyleEditor's wiki](https://github.com/nptr/msstyleEditor/wiki/Window-Frames)). For an app drawing its own, Microsoft names E921 minimize, E922 maximize, E923 restore and E8BB close ([title bar design](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/basics/titlebar-design)), from Segoe Fluent Icons on Windows 11 and Segoe MDL2 Assets on Windows 10, which Firefox draws its own window buttons with at 10 CSS pixels ([browser.css](https://github.com/mozilla-firefox/firefox/blob/main/browser/themes/windows/browser.css)).
- **Windows' sizes are copied rather than published.** Microsoft gives no pixel sizes for Win32 caption buttons. Firefox, Windows Terminal and tauri-controls make each 46 wide, and Chromium 45 with a 1-pixel gap. Windows 11's DWM makes them 47, 46 and 47 at 100 percent, read from a Windhawk mod reverse-engineered on 25H2. Windows reports a shown window's real button rectangles through `DWMWA_CAPTION_BUTTON_BOUNDS` and `WM_GETTITLEBARINFOEX`.
- **Windows' colors.** Windows 10's close hover is #E81123 with a white glyph and pressed that red at alpha 0x98, and minimize and maximize take the glyph's color at alpha 0x1A on hover and 0x33 pressed, from Chromium's `native_chrome_color_mixer_win.cc`. Windows 11's close hover is #C42B1C and pressed that red at 90 percent, from Windows Terminal's `MinMaxCloseControl.xaml`. No Microsoft document states either red.
- **Linux puts close at the top right everywhere but elementary.** Ubuntu shows minimize, maximize and close on the right; vanilla GNOME on Fedora close alone; KDE Plasma 6, Mint's Cinnamon, Xubuntu and Raspberry Pi OS minimize, maximize and close on the right; elementary close on the left and maximize on the right. GTK's `gtk-decoration-layout` setting carries the user's own order, and fuji's Rust could read it.
- **Ubuntu's own buttons are libadwaita's**: circles 24 CSS pixels across, 13 apart, the outermost centered 23 pixels in from the right edge and down from the top of a 46 or 47 pixel header bar, the circle the text color at 10, 15 and 30 percent, with Yaru's or Adwaita's symbolic SVGs for the glyphs. Electron's Linux buttons have drawn this way since July 2026 ([freedesktop_nav_button_provider.cc](https://github.com/electron/electron/blob/main/shell/browser/ui/views/freedesktop_nav_button_provider.cc)).
- **Mac video players keep the title bar and hide it.** mpv's window is always titled, and `--border=no` hides its title bar with `titlebarAppearsTransparent` and `.fullSizeContentView` (mpv's `video/out/mac/window.swift`). IINA's main window is the same, and Electron's frameless window starts titled. AppKit hands WindowServer a corner mask, and WindowServer clips the window as it composites it ([mac-window-corners](https://github.com/mansarip/mac-window-corners)), so a titled window's corners cost the video nothing inside the application.
- **A borderless window rounds with public API**: the window non-opaque with a clear background, and a layer's `cornerRadius` and `masksToBounds`. Nobody has measured what a non-opaque window costs WindowServer, the radius has to be written down per release, and the shadow follows only after `invalidateShadow`.
- **The radius by release, from reports, with no Apple figure.** Sequoia 10 points. Tahoe 16 for a window with a title bar and no toolbar, 20 with a compact toolbar and 26 with a unified one, where Apple says "Windows with toolbars now use a larger radius… Titlebar-only windows retain a smaller corner radius" (WWDC25 session 310). Golden Gate 20 for every window, by a forum report. No public API reports the radius before macOS 27's `NSView.cornerConfiguration`.
