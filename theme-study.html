Theme Study

A copy of fuji's grays to change freely, standing apart from fuji: Zed's, from its One Light and One Dark themes, the numbered grays, which carry Tailwind's lightness in Zed's tints, and the two named for their job, copied from desktop/src/style.css on 2026-11-18. The numbered grays beside Tailwind's

Each row is one step at one lightness: Tailwind's grays, copied from version 4.3.3, then the numbered grays above as light and dark draw them. Each cell reads its OKLCH lightness, chroma and hue, with no hue where the chroma is under 0.002, too little to lean any way.

The numbered grays every 25, with the named colors on them

Each strip is the numbered grays in one mode, from 50 to 950 in steps of 25. The steps in bold are the eleven written above; the ones between are the study's own, each on a straight line in OKLab between the written steps on either side, and not Tailwind's, which has none. Above each strip, every named color sits at the number its lightness falls at on the same lines, with a swatch of its own color to set beside the strip's under it, since a name matches the strip in lightness and can differ in tint. A name whose tint is 0.005 or more from the strip's, measured across a and b in OKLab at the same lightness, gives its hue beside the strip's on its mark. Names that come to one color share a mark. Point at a segment for its number and reading, or at a mark for its reading beside the strip's.

Below each, the same strip neutralized: Tailwind's neutral exactly, with no tint at any step, and each name at its own lightness with its tint taken out, which is the gray given beside its number. Its ground, its words and its lines are neutralized the same way, so the strip shows the look with no blue in it.
