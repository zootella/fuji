Button Color

Candidate palettes for the bar's drawings, each laid out as a box of crayons: every hue the palette has at one step, in order around the color wheel, each with a drawing in it at 24 and 16 css pixels. Below each box, the toolbar and an edit menu, where Back, Forward and Up take the palette's blue, Folders its yellow, Delete its red and Undo its green, the four named so far, and every other drawing stays in the text's own color. Light is on white and dark on black, the grounds the bar stands on under Windows' title bar. Point at a button or a row to see it lit and lifted, as FujiBar draws it.
