# Secure erase

## The feature

- **Delete erases the file for good.** The file manager fuji is growing will carry the Edit menu Explorer, Finder and the orthodox file managers share, cut, copy and paste among it, and beside the command that moves a file to the Trash or the Recycle Bin, Delete. Fuji's Delete is a secure erase: when the user deletes a file with fuji, the file is really gone.
- **It comes on out of the box, and fast.** It follows the standalone erasers and the research behind them, and leaves behind their weight: the user gets the peace of mind and pays nothing extra for it in time or in what they have to know.
- **It erases the files the user deletes, and nothing else.** Fuji offers no whole-drive work: no scrub of free space, and nothing for files deleted earlier or by another program.
- **It is the promise on the home page, applied to deleting.** Fuji is designed with privacy and precision in mind, and a Delete that really erases is that design carried to the end of a file's life.
- **The standalone erasers ask a lot of their user.** They are involved and frightening: a separate program to find and install, a long computer science paper behind them, detailed settings, and a confusing manual. With fuji the user needs none of that to know a deleted file is gone.
- **Old collections, new computers.** Fuji lets the user browse collections that may be huge, varied and old, and it assumes current consumer hardware for both security and speed: Windows 10 and 11 PCs, recent Macs, and the popular Linux desktops.
- **The storage it is designed for is today's.** The file systems are APFS, NTFS and the popular Linux desktop file systems. The media are the SSD built into a MacBook, a spinning-platter USB drive of around 10 TB like those sold on Amazon, and the free USB thumb drive from an event that has sat at the bottom of a backpack ever since.
- **Its time is now, though the file manager is a ways out.** Fuji is built broad from the bottom up, and the sprint just finished was the security of parsing media files, which erasing a file sits right beside.

## The four guidelines

The article states these as a bulleted list, and the choices fuji makes answer to them.

- **Permanently set**, with no setting to forget.
- **Configuration free**, with no complex choices to weigh.
- **Secure**, even against a highly sophisticated adversary.
- **Designed for current consumer hardware**, the file systems and media above.

## The article

- **Erase is written, its history and fuji's half**, and the page's Delete, Move to Trash and move across drives are built to match it.
- **It keeps to the engineering, the physics and the cryptography**, and says nothing of who wants a secret kept or found, or what the law allows.
- **Wright, Kleiman and Sundhar's 2008 paper stays out**, since a court has since found its lead author a forger on a grand scale.
